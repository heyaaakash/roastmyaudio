Setup & troubleshooting ↗
YOUR VOICE STAYS CLOSE

Local by design.

Your browser sends recorded audio to this Flask server. When you run it on this computer, transcription and optional cleanup happen here.

Audio WAVs, transcripts, vocabulary, and settings are saved on this computer. Models download from Hugging Face on first use and are cached locally. Your scratchpad and appearance preference are saved with the workspace, independently of its window or server port. Native clipboard context is off by default and can be enabled in Preferences.

No analytics or external fonts are loaded by this interface. Don't expose the unauthenticated server to a public network. Delete a recording to remove its saved audio and history entry; dismissed recordings remain stored.
